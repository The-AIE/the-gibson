# Contributor Assignment Agreement

**DRAFT v0.1 — 2026-10-21 — not legal advice. Have IP counsel review before first signature.**

Applies to: ConferenceOS, The Gibson, Chatterbuilt, and any other repository whose
`CONTRIBUTING.md` links to this agreement ("**Projects**").
Assignee: **Peripety Labs LLC**, a [STATE] limited liability company ("**Peripety**").

You ("**Contributor**") agree with Peripety as follows. This agreement covers every
Contribution you submit to a Project from the date you sign, until you or Peripety
gives written notice that it ends (notice never affects Contributions already made).

## 1. Definitions

- **Contribution**: any code, documentation, design, product definition, test, data,
  or other work you submit to a Project (pull request, issue, comment, file, or
  otherwise), including work produced with AI assistance under your direction.
- **Open-source License**: the license the Project publishes under at the time of
  your Contribution (for The Gibson: Apache-2.0).

## 2. Assignment

You assign to Peripety, worldwide and for the full term of protection, all right,
title and interest, including copyright, in each Contribution. Where assignment is
not permitted by law, you grant Peripety an exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable,
worldwide, royalty-free, sublicensable license to do everything an owner could do.
You waive, and agree not to assert, moral rights to the extent the law allows.

## 3. License back to you

Peripety grants you a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free
license to use, copy, modify, and distribute your own Contributions for any purpose
(including in your own projects and portfolio).

## 4. Peripety's commitment to the community

Peripety will keep making each Contribution available to the public under an
OSI-approved open-source license (the Open-source License above, or a later
OSI-approved license). Peripety may additionally offer the Project under other terms
(for example commercial or managed-service terms). This section survives any later
change in Peripety's ownership.

## 5. Patents

You grant Peripety and every recipient of the Project a perpetual, worldwide,
non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable patent license under patent claims you
control that are necessarily infringed by your Contribution alone or combined with
the Project. If you sue anyone alleging a Contribution infringes your patent,
that license ends for you as to that suit.

## 6. Your promises

You confirm that: (a) each Contribution is your original work or you have the right
to submit it; (b) you have the right to enter this agreement — if your employer or
a school has rights in your work, you have obtained written permission or a waiver
and will send Peripety a copy on request; (c) you will tell Peripety about any
third-party material, with its license, in the pull request that includes it;
(d) you reviewed and take responsibility for AI-assisted output, and disclose AI
assistance by commit trailer (`Co-Authored-By`) or in the PR description;
(e) you did not include secrets or personal data of others.

Except for these promises, Contributions are provided "as is" with no warranty.

## 7. Sign-off still required

Every commit must also carry a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off
(`git commit -s`). The DCO and this agreement work together; if they conflict,
this agreement controls on ownership and the DCO controls on provenance.

## 8. Credit

Peripety will keep your name in the repository history and a contributors list
(`CONTRIBUTORS.md`) unless you ask to be omitted. Assignment does not remove credit.

## 9. General

Governing law: [STATE]. This agreement is the entire agreement on its subject. Peripety
may assign it to a successor that agrees to Section 4. Electronic signature is binding.
You are an independent contributor — this agreement creates no employment,
agency, or payment obligation, and you are not owed compensation unless Peripety
agrees in a separate writing.
