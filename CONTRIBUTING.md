# Contributing

Thanks for helping with The Gibson.

## Licensing of contributions

Before your first pull request is merged, sign the
[Contributor Assignment Agreement](./CLA.md). You assign copyright in your
contributions to Peripety Labs LLC and receive a license back to use your own
work; Peripety commits to keep every contribution available under an
OSI-approved open-source license ([`Apache-2.0`](./LICENSE)). Your name stays in
[`CONTRIBUTORS.md`](./CONTRIBUTORS.md) and the git history.

## Sign-off and AI assistance

Sign off every commit (`git commit -s`) to certify the
[Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/). AI-assisted
work is welcome when you direct and review it; disclose it with a
`Co-Authored-By` trailer or in the PR description.
