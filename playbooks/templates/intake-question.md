# Intake question (planner → Operator)

One question at a time. Business vocabulary only.

```
QUESTION: {{single_business_question}}
WHY I ASK: {{how_the_answer_changes_what_we_build}}
EXAMPLES:  {{optional_example_answers_so_they_are_not_stuck}}
REPLY:     in your own words is perfect
```

## How to use this

Never stack three questions. Never ask about databases, frameworks, or APIs.

### Example

```
QUESTION: When a customer books, should they get an email confirmation from you?
WHY I ASK: That decides whether we connect email now or later.
EXAMPLES:  "Yes, always" / "Only if they check a box" / "No, SMS only"
REPLY:     in your own words is perfect
```
