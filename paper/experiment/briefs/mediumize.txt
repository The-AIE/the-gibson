# Sponsors and community partners need multiple contacts, not one embedded contact

Requested 2026-08-08.

## The constraint today

`Sponsor` embeds exactly **one** contact as flat columns:

```prisma
companyName   String
contactName   String
contactEmail  String
contactPhone  String?
```

One sponsor therefore has one contact, by construction. There is no way to record the person who signed, the marketing lead who sends the logo, and the on-site staffer who shows up — which is the normal shape of a real sponsorship.

## Related to #1215

That issue notes the mirror-image problem from the CRM side: `CrmContact.companyName` is **free text, not a relation**, so a contact is not linked to the `Sponsor` record their deal belongs to. Two people from the same sponsor cannot be seen as one account.

Both point at the same fix: a proper **many-contacts-to-one-organization** relation, with `CrmContact` as the person record and `Sponsor` as the company. The embedded columns become the primary/billing contact at most, or migrate into the relation entirely.

## Considerations

- **Roles matter.** Billing, marketing, on-site, signatory — an invoice should go to the billing contact, not whoever was entered first.
- **Migration is not a drop.** The existing `contactName`/`contactEmail`/`contactPhone` values are real data and must become contact records, not be discarded. The additive path is: add the relation, backfill from the flat columns, and only later consider retiring them — the schema guard blocks non-additive changes without an explicit coordinator exception (#1170) for good reason.
- Community partners should use the same model rather than a parallel one.
